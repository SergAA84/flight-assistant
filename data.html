Time zones — ODbL 1.0

Contains information from timezone-boundary-builder 2026e, © OpenStreetMap contributors, made available under the Open Database License 1.0.

The app uses an adapted database: polygons simplified with a 0.005° tolerance (topology preserved), coordinates rounded to five decimals. The complete adapted database is available here: timezones.json (ODbL 1.0). The file and the bundled world_geography.json carry machine-readable license metadata (license, license_uri, attribution, source_version, modifications; per-table licenses object in the bundled file).
