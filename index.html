Flight Assistant

Offline iPhone companion for passengers: live speed, altitude and position from GPS, favorite airports with distance and time estimates, automatic flight history and a 3D Earth globe. Works in airplane mode.

Русский: офлайн-помощник пассажира — скорость, высота, координаты, аэропорты, история полётов и 3D-глобус в авиарежиме.
Italiano: assistente offline per passeggeri — velocità, altitudine, posizione, aeroporti, cronologia voli e globo 3D in modalità aereo.

Informational use only. Not for aircraft operation or navigation.
