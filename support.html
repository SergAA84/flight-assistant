Frequently asked questions

Does it work in airplane mode? Yes. GPS works without a cellular or Wi-Fi connection. All maps, airports and geography are built in.

No signal on board? Hold the iPhone close to a window. The aircraft cabin can block satellite signals; reception varies by seat and aircraft.

Why was my flight not recorded? Flights are recorded automatically only when sustained flight evidence is observed. Open the app before takeoff. Enable “Keep tracking with screen off” to continue in the background.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone? Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account.
